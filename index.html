    Identity

    Stats

    Value is the raw stat from your sheet; the mod is what gets rolled. Mods feed checks, attacks and melee.

    Combat

    Initiative = badass rank + SPD mod + misc. Movement = 3 + SPD mod + misc. Melee = melee die + DMG mod + misc.

    Checks

    Total = stat mod + misc (gear, background, traits). The dice tab buttons use these totals.

    Vitals

    Use the steppers mid-fight; the header bars follow along. Damage shields first, like the game intends.
